ShellShifter 1.4.235

For Maya 2025 and 2027 on Windows.

- Organized the repository: plugin files in ShellShifter/, instructions in
  docs/, and release building files in tools/.
- Shortened release titles and installation archive names.
- Updates accepts both the new archive names and the earlier beta filenames.

Choose the archive for your Maya version, extract it completely, and drag
install_shellshifter.py into Maya's 3D viewport. Save the Shelf and keep the
extracted folder in place. Save your scene and restart Maya after an update.

Установка: скачайте ZIP для своей версии Maya, полностью распакуйте его и
перетащите install_shellshifter.py в 3D-вьюпорт. Сохраните Shelf. После обновления
сохраните сцену и перезапустите Maya.
